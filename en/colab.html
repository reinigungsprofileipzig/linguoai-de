The short browser guide

Start LinguoAI in Google Colab.

Open the prepared Notebook directly. The startup cell sets up LinguoAI and then shows the link to your protected web interface.

Start in Google Colab ↗

A Google account is required. Setup runs in Colab, rather than on your computer. Allow time for installation and model downloads on your first run.

That's what you need
  • a Google account
  • a short test video
  • an active internet connection

The default settings are ready. A GPU and Google Drive are optional.

Availability and costs

Google restricts the predominant use of external web interfaces in free Colab runtimes without a positive Compute unit balance. Check the Colab terms before starting.

Step by step

Three steps. Your result.

  1. 01
    Open and start the Notebook

    Click “Start in Google Colab” above and sign in to Google. In the Notebook, choose “Runtime → Run all”. The startup cell installs LinguoAI; wait for the link and credentials to appear.

  2. 02
    Choose video and language

    Open the generated Gradio link and sign in with the credentials shown. Upload a short video, choose the target language, and start the job. For your first test, you can leave the other settings as they are.

  3. 03
    Save your results

    Download the newly dubbed video and both subtitle files, or save them to Google Drive. Keep the Notebook cell running during processing; the link and temporary files are no longer available after the session ends.

YOUR RESULT

Video and subtitles.

The new audio track completely replaces the existing sound, including music and background noise. Lip sync and automatic matching of multiple speakers are not included.

YOUR DATA

Choose carefully.

Colab processes files in a temporary Google environment. Online translators and online voices receive the texts needed for their tasks. Only use videos and voices for which you have the necessary rights.

When you need more.

The direct Notebook link does not work

You can still download the Notebook and open it in Google Colab using “File → Open Notebook → Upload”.

Download Notebook ↓
Use multiple videos or Google Drive

Multiple videos are processed one after another. You can upload them together or enter relative MyDrive file paths, one per line. To use Drive, first enable the corresponding option in the Notebook.

Gradio allows up to 5 GB per file by default. Google Drive is an alternative for large videos; the available runtime and storage remain limited.

Advanced options: voices and Cloudflare

You do not need your own domain or a tunnel token for the standard setup. Local voices and your own Cloudflare Tunnel are additional options.
